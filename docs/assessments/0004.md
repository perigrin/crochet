## What blocks

Both participants raised the first two independently, from different briefs,
without knowledge of each other.

**1. The citation check contradicts the section plan at landing.** The accepted
decisions are 0001, 0002 and 0003. The Sections table cites `[0001]` and
`[0002]` only; 0003 appears in prose about `amends` and in References, never as
a section source. So on landing either `sh xt/run.sh` goes red — which is AC8 —
or the synthesis gains a citation of 0003 that exists to satisfy the check
rather than carry a claim, the exact failure 0004 names in its own Completeness
paragraph. And 0003's substance cannot easily go there: the text 0004 absorbs
verbatim explicitly refuses to carry it, "policy rather than architecture...
This document carries the pipeline's shape and stops there". The same collision
recurs when 0004 itself flips to `accepted`, since nothing cites 0004 either.

**2. Acceptance criterion 6 is already green on an untouched tree.**

```
$ sh xt/run.sh xt/fixture 2>&1 | grep -q 'cites' && echo GREEN
GREEN
```

The matching line is the *existing* cite-symbols check: `FAIL:
docs/decisions/0004-citation.md cites a line number, not a symbol:
graph.go:439`. The criterion passes whether or not the check it exists to prove
is ever written. The repository's own defect class, inside the verification of
the decision that adds checks against it. The other seven criteria are red and
none is unrunnable. One-line fix: a distinguishing substring that does not
contain `cites a line number`.

**3. The criteria set is the count the document rejects.** A
`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` whose entire content is `[0001] [0002] [0003]` satisfies
all eight acceptance criteria. That is the straw man 0004 constructs and
dismisses — "a document listing every decision's headings at the bottom would
satisfy any such count while reflecting none of them" — reproduced in the
document's own verification. Seven criteria check placement and wiring; the
deliverable is checked by `test -f`. Since `git-zhi-verify` runs these at
`milestone edit --state complete`, the milestone can close green on a stub.

**4. Doc-first has no trigger for an accepted-but-unbuilt decision.** The check
fires on `state: accepted`. Doc-first obliges only "a decision's implementing
pull request", which an unbuilt decision does not have. So an accepted-but-unbuilt
decision must appear as a present-tense claim in the document `CLAUDE.md:17`
imports into every agent invocation — which is the failure 0001 exists to
prevent, and 0001 names accepted-but-not-built as its contribution over ADR and
RFD. 0004 provides no form for this case. 0005 and 0007 are already placeholders.

**5. The trigger nominated to schedule assess cannot fire.** 0004 says "`git zhi
docs health` reporting drift on the document is the trigger to run assess over
it." Drift is computed from the document's **mtime**. Measured on this worktree:

```
Summary: 9 docs checked, all current
docs/architecture/plugin-structure.md   mtime 2026-09-21T14:25:57
docs/assessments/rfc-0003.md            mtime 2026-09-21T14:25:57
docs/assessments/walkthrough-assess.md  mtime 2026-09-21T14:25:57
```

Every mtime is the checkout timestamp, identical across all nine. In any fresh
clone or worktree — which is where CI runs and where every dispatched agent
works — churn is 0 and drift is NONE for every document, permanently. So
"assess carries correctness" degrades to "someone remembers".

`development-workflow.md` already records two ways this tool reports all-clear
over nothing. This is a third, and it is a defect in git-zhi rather than in
0004; it belongs in `docs/requests/` whatever 0004 does.

The same mechanism is mistuned in the other direction too: if the trigger did
fire it would fire on ordinary `skills/` churn, and `assess.md` Step 0
mandatorily delegates to `crochet:discernment` — so each firing summons a
multi-agent session. 0004's one Open Question is the one that would tune it, and
it says "Neither has been run."

## What else was raised

**Collisions with decisions in force.**

- **0001's second acceptance criterion goes permanently red.**
  `0001:566` is `test -n "$(ls docs/architecture/*.md 2>/dev/null)"`. 0004
  removes that directory and asserts `! test -e docs/architecture` as its own
  AC3. Nothing detects it: `xt/run.sh` does not execute decision criteria. This
  is the shape `coding-conventions.md` already records biting 0002.
- **"Two rules change; the rest holds" understates the blast radius.**
  `docs/architecture` appears in 0001 at lines 97, 112, 157, 387, 479, 502 and
  566. Six are archive prose, fine to leave stale under 0001's own as-of-date
  rule — but a reader is told two rules moved and finds seven sites with no way
  to sort deliberate staleness from oversight. One sentence closes it.
- **0004 removes the damping 0001 named as its mechanism.** 0001: "stingy with
  live, generous with archive... almost nothing is promoted out of it, and that
  asymmetry, not a threshold, is what damps the feedback loop." 0004 institutes
  obligatory promotion. That may be the right trade; it is a third changed rule
  the amendment section does not declare, and it should be argued rather than
  left for a future reader to find as a contradiction between two documents both
  in force.

**The runnable-criteria question, which is not 0004's to settle.** `spec-lens`
blocks on 0004 carrying eight runnable criteria at all, citing
`coding-conventions.md`'s "A decision does not carry runnable acceptance
criteria" (`d89e288`, 2026-09-20). A third session reviewing the merged branch
argued the reverse from the same evidence: that the sentence contradicts
accepted 0001, which *requires* runnable criteria — "one runnable command per
checkbox line in a paren-wrapped backtick span" — and that 0003 does not settle
the division the sentence cites it for.

The evidence that the broad rule is not the operative one is that nothing
follows it: at `6a18943`, 0001 carries 8 runnable criteria, 0003 carries 33 and
0004 carries 8. Only 0002 was stripped, and 0002 was the one whose criteria had
gone permanently red.

**This gates 0004 and is not a finding 0004 can answer**, because 0004 is the
decision that amends 0001. It is recorded here and referred out.

**Checks that fail open.** Both proposed checks iterate what the synthesis
cites, so zero citations yields zero findings — indistinguishable from a perfect
document. The runner's three existing per-file loops open `[ -f "$f" ] ||
continue`, and `xt/run.sh` carries a comment recording that exact bite. Both
checks must read `$ROOT/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`; the runner carries two comments
about that trap and 0004 never says `$ROOT`.

**The check is weaker than the document says.** 0004 calls it weak, "catches a
decision cited nowhere and nothing finer". Because the citation form is `[NNNN]`
*linked to the file*, the real predicate is "the four-digit string appears in
the file" — satisfied by a link, a code fence, a References entry, or a sentence
reading "0003 is deliberately not synthesised here". The document's written
description of its own weakness is what it offers a reader in place of a
stronger check, so understating it matters.

**`xt/fixture/expected` is not in scope and it is what keeps the new checks
honest.** It declares `# count: 9`, has that count checked against its entries,
carries a "9 of 26 sites" ledger saying "Recount when a site is added", and is
the only thing that detects a check going quiet. Two fixture cases landing
without two `expected` entries means the self-test passes while proving nothing
about the new checks.

**Smaller.** Retargeting the CONTRIBUTING link falsifies the sentence two lines
below it ("Every link here points at a directory that holds a file"), and Scope
of Change does not mention it. Three by-path citations to
`plugin-structure.md` go dangling — `0003:55`, `0003:566`, `0006:123` — two of
them inside an accepted decision, in the class `coding-conventions.md` states it
cannot detect. 0004 says "0003 is `proposed`" and builds an argument on it; 0003
is accepted, so the paragraph is moot rather than wrong. The table row
"compiler, not runtime" names a concept no decision decides. AC2 shells out to
`python3` where a `grep` does the job in the repository's own language. The
always-imported context grows with the series, compelled by a check rather than
by editorial judgment.

## Standing aside

Recorded with grounds, not blocking, as each participant declared it.

- **`codebase-lens`, on a rung-2 completeness check.** "A decision landed
  without touching the synthesis" *is* mechanically checkable — for each
  decision whose Scope of Change names `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, does a commit
  carrying its `Implements:` trailer touch that file? `xt/run.sh` already reads
  those trailers. 0004 conflates that with "is the synthesis *good*", and only
  the second is uncheckable. It believes this is right and buildable; it accepts
  that the decision's judgment is defensible and does not hold on it.
- **`spec-lens`, on unbounded growth of the always-imported document.** It turns
  on how long the synthesis actually is, which the author knows and the
  participant does not. If the intent is a short claims-with-citations document,
  the concern dissolves.
