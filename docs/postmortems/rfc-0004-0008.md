# Milestone rfc-0004-0008 Postmortem

11/11 done, 15 commits, one issue reopened once. Verify 70/71, the one
outstanding criterion being unsatisfiable by design rather than unmet — see
below. Both review lenses passed after two rounds each.

The assessment that preceded this milestone has its own postmortem in
`rfc-0004-assessment.md`, written while that gate was still open. This one
covers refinement through review.

## What Worked Well

**Every gate found something no criterion could.** The synthesis passed all ten
of its acceptance criteria at the moment its gate opened, where `git zhi`
verified 10/10 — and the coverage lens still found one of 0004's ten Problem
Statement rows two-thirds discharged, and then found that the absorption the
teardown was about to authorise was incomplete. The quality lens found four
defects in shell that passed every criterion written for it. **Nine of the
eleven issues were green on every criterion at the moment their gate opened,
and the gates still had work to do.**

**The live-document lens caught its own delivery on its first run.** 0008 gives
`crochet:review` a step that reads live documents against the diff. The first
branch it ran on was the branch that created it, and it found a sentence that
branch had added to `development-workflow.md` — "The five above run", false for
two of the five, in a document loaded into every session. Nothing in either
suite could have caught that: it is a judgment performed by an agent, and 0008
argues in terms that a mechanical check is unavailable for this rule.

**Running the criteria found what reading them did not.** Chain-review had nine
rounds of reading across two decisions and reached unity. Execution then found
three defects in the first hour, all by running things: a decoy in
`xt/fixture/CLAUDE.md` that cited the decision it was written to leave uncited,
and two prose literals that wrapped across line breaks where their criteria grep
unsqueezed. The fixture caught the first on its first hand-built run.

**The mechanical backstop was proved in both directions before it shipped.**
Stubbed, both declared fixture entries redden with "that check has gone quiet".
Live, removing a decision's only citation gives `accepted and implemented, but
cited nowhere in the live layer: 0008`. An arm that can never pass looks exactly
like a correct red on day one, and reverting the body is the only thing that
tells them apart.

## What Went Wrong

**A dispatch instruction caused three tree mutations and a detached HEAD.** The
first review subagent was told to restore the shared working tree with `git
checkout --` after probing it. That makes correctness depend on a cleanup step
running in a tree another agent is committing to. It left a probe in
`xt/run.sh`, deleted `xt/fixture/CLAUDE.md`, and something moved HEAD to an
earlier commit — one commit landed on the detached head and was recovered by
cherry-pick after confirming the working tree was byte-identical to the branch
tip. Nothing was lost, and only because `git add` named explicit paths.

The instruction was at fault, not the agent. The reviewer that extracted its
subject with `git archive` into a scratch repository produced the only
measurements from that window worth trusting. **Scratch extraction is the
instruction to give**, and every later dispatch said so.

**A criterion this repository's own rules forbid shipped in the plan.** Issue
`01a0c61f`, which authored the synthesis, carried "nothing was removed or retargeted in this issue" as
`test -f docs/architecture/plugin-structure.md && ...` — a claim about a diff,
written as a claim about the working tree. It was true when the issue ran and
became unsatisfiable when the teardown landed, so reopening the issue for an
unrelated repair left it permanently red. Satisfying it would mean reverting
correct work. Filed as its own issue: a criterion asserts a property of the
change, not of the tree.

**An accepted decision was edited in the branch that wrote the rule against
it.** Removing 0001's defective criterion is authorised by 0004. Adding fourteen
lines of prose explaining the removal is not, and 0001's own rule — restated in
this milestone's synthesis two commits earlier — is that an accepted entry is
immutable in content and append-only in status. The coverage lens caught it and
the block came back out. The removal was never silent: `amended-by: [0004]` in
frontmatter, 0004's own text, `coding-conventions.md`'s worked example, and the
commit trailer all record it.

**Four defects shipped into `xt/run.sh` that every criterion passed.** Two made
a green run untrustworthy. A typo in a `CLAUDE.md` import was silently skipped
by `[ -f "$f" ] || continue` — the exact shape the runner's own comment condemns
two checks above, written immediately after reading it. And a space in `$ROOT`
or `$TMPDIR` word-split the live set, so `sh xt/run.sh` with no arguments
reported a working check had gone quiet, and a spaced root reported five
decisions uncited. Both reachable from documented invocations. Both found by a
lens constructing cases nobody had tested.

## What We Learned

**The vacuous pass has five variants and running criteria at `HEAD` reaches
one.** The catalogue, as it now stands:

| | Reached by |
|---|---|
| green at `HEAD` | the scan — nineteen found in chain-review |
| green via a prerequisite issue | reading the chain, and a static `paths:` rule |
| unsatisfiable from the start | reverting the work and reading the red's cause |
| red only through its anchor | splitting criteria on `&&` and running each conjunct |
| honest when written, unrunnable after a successor | nothing — found by a verify regression |

The fifth is new here. The fourth was found during chain-review by both lenses
independently and is why `xt/fixture/expected`'s entries name sub-causes rather
than a shared literal.

**A green-today negation is not a defect; what it asserts decides.** A negation
guarding a failure an implementer might *introduce* is correctly green before
and after, and still reddens on the failure it names. A negation asserting a
*transition* is broken if it never matched. Neither redness nor a criterion
count separates them. Four negations in this chain were the guarding shape and
correct; one was not.

**A check with no output is not evidence of a clean subject until it has been
seen to produce output over a dirty one.** Three times in this milestone a text
tool did not do what its author assumed and the result passed without checking:
BSD `sed` rejecting `\|` in a basic regex, BSD `sed` not expanding `\n` in a
replacement — which made a conjunct scan print "scan complete" having examined
nothing — and `grep`'s case sensitivity against a capitalised ledger. All three
failed open, and two were inside checks written to catch things failing open.

**A gate whose subject the delivery deletes has a window nothing knows about.**
The absorption gap was findable only because `plugin-structure.md` was still in
the tree when the lens read it. One issue later and "nothing is left over" is
unfalsifiable rather than false. The chain's dependency order works against
catching it, because the teardown must depend on the authoring. The cheaper
answer is probably that a deletion's own diff contains the deleted file, so a
reviewer reading `git show` rather than the working tree always has the
comparison — but that is a decision to assess, not a rule to invent at the end of
an execution run.

## Addendum: the review gate found three more things after the merge

The coverage lens had reviewed `6ffd884..bb44c17` and said so; the three commits
that answered its findings, and this document, were unreviewed by anything. It
flagged that itself rather than being asked. **A gate that reviews everything
except its own corrections has a hole exactly where corrections get made** — at
the end of a long run, under time pressure. That run found:

**The check-family count was wrong in two files, by the commit that was careful
about counts.** The `xt/run.sh` rework added a twelfth banner that emits two
findings of its own, making ten families. The same commit recounted the site
ledger from 30 to 33 and its message says the previous count "had not counted
the third" hard exit — and the family count sat one line above in the same file
and moved by nothing. Recounting one ledger is not a habit that transfers to the
next one; only checking transfers.

**Nothing defined what a check family is.** Nine and ten are both derivable from
twelve banners depending on which two you exclude, and the exclusions lived
nowhere. `xt/run.sh` now states the arithmetic, so the number can be checked
rather than guessed.

**And correcting it reddens a second criterion, for the same reason as the
first.** `grep -q 'Nine check families' xt/run.sh` was a positive criterion of
the backstop issue. It was honest when written and became wrong when a later
change legitimately moved the count — the identical shape as "nothing was
removed in this issue", generalised from a path to a number. The catalogue's
fifth variant now has two instances from one milestone, which is the argument
for the constraint filed as #16 rather than a one-off repair.

**One four-word overstatement.** "This is last because nothing about it can be
automated" — part of the review step *is* automated, and the same document names
the backstop that does it. What resists automation is the judgment, not the
step, and 0008 is careful about exactly that distinction. The sentence was
looser than the decision it rests on.

**And a reference a reader cannot follow.** This document cited issue `b892`;
git-zhi's short id is the *first* segment of a uuid, so `git zhi issue show b892`
does not resolve. It is `01a0c61f`. A citation that only its author can
dereference is the same defect as a stale path, at a smaller scale.
